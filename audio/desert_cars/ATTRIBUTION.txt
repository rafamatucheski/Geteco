These are real idle recordings used as character references for fictional cars.
They are not recordings of the fictional Coyote or Bison, nor complete sampled
RPM banks. Other engine layers remain the project's offline combustion synthesis.
Exact files, preparation and SHA256 checksums are recorded in SOURCES.json.
