# Ocean surface

Generated with the built-in Image Gen tool on 2026-10-22. Final project asset: `assets/ui/map/ocean-surface.png`. Used only as a static, world-anchored material behind the authoritative map geography; it contains no land or navigation data. Mipmap import and linear sampling support minification during zoom.

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: seamless tiling top-down OCEAN SURFACE TEXTURE for the world map of a grounded urban PC game Harbor. Square 1024x1024. Orthographic looking straight down at open deep ocean, no horizon or perspective. Dark desaturated navy blue and subtle teal, very fine realistic wind-driven wavelets and coherent soft ocean swell, sparse tiny pale blue specular crests. Should immediately read as sea water while being quiet enough for light cartographic roads and map icons over it. Base colors approximately #102b3a #163b4a #245064 with highlights no brighter than #527781. Material texture, no text, no symbols, NO coastline, NO land, NO islands, NO boats, NO sea creatures, NO grids, NO large white foam or bright reflections. Uniform lighting with no center spotlight and no vignette. Seamless edges so it can repeat without visible seams. Realistic satellite-like water but restrained, moderately low contrast, no marble or stone appearance.

Menu pictograms use supplied [Lucide SVG assets](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide) under their included ISC license (`assets/ui/harbor-menu/LICENSE`).
